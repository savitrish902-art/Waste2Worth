# ♻️ Waste2Worth

### Turning Waste into Worth — One Action at a Time

Waste2Worth is a sustainability-focused web platform designed to encourage responsible waste management by making reuse, recycling, donation, and proper disposal easier and more engaging.

Our goal is to help individuals make environmentally responsible choices while building a community around sustainable living.

## 🌍 The Problem

Waste is often discarded without considering whether it can be reused, recycled, or donated. People may also lack clear guidance and motivation to adopt sustainable habits consistently.

## 💡 Our Solution

Waste2Worth aims to make waste management more accessible through practical guidance, engaging challenges, rewards, and environmental impact tracking.

## ✨ Key Features

* **Waste Management Guidance:** Encourage reuse, recycling, donation, and responsible disposal.
* **Rewards and Badges:** Recognize sustainable actions and progress.
* **Community Challenges:** Encourage people to participate in environmental activities.
* **Leaderboards and Streaks:** Motivate consistent eco-friendly habits.
* **Environmental Impact Tracking:** Help users understand the positive impact of their actions.
* **User-Friendly Interface:** Make sustainability easier to understand and practice.

*Feature availability depends on the functionality implemented in the current version.*

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Open or download this repository.
2. Ensure `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js` are in the correct locations.
3. Open `index.html` in a web browser.
